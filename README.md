# Customer Behavior & Sales Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze customer purchasing behavior, sales performance, product trends, and revenue growth.

## Dashboard Preview
![Dashboard Overview](Images/dashboard_overview.png)

## Business Objectives
- Analyze overall sales performance and year-over-year growth.
- Identify top-performing products and categories.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior.
- Analyze payment method preferences.
- Evaluate customer retention and repeat purchase rates.
- Identify sales trends across customer demographics.

## Key KPIs
- Total Sales
- Sales Last Year
- YoY Growth
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Repeat Customer Rate
- Top Product
- Top Category

## Dashboard Features
- Sales trends and cumulative sales analysis.
- Product-level revenue analysis.
- Category-wise sales comparison.
- Payment method analysis.
- Customer-level sales table.
- Customer age group segmentation.
- Interactive filters for date, year, city, and product category.

## Tools & Technologies
- Power BI
- DAX
- Power Query
- Data Modeling
- Data Visualization

## Business Insights
- Home is the leading product category.
- Curtains are the top-performing product.
- Customer purchasing patterns vary across age groups.
- Repeat customers contribute to overall sales performance.
